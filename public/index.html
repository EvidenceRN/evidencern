Prototype v0.5.2. Sources retrieved live from PubMed (U.S. National Library of Medicine) via NLM E-utilities. Retracted records and retraction notices are excluded automatically using PubMed publication metadata and comments/corrections links; expressions of concern, errata, and linked updates are flagged on source cards. Main answers and follow-ups use heuristic post-generation citation checks. Synthesis is AI-generated from the retrieved abstracts only unless Deep Read retrieves PubMed Central full text. Evidence-type labels follow the Melnyk & Fineout-Overholt hierarchy, inferred from publication metadata. They indicate study design, not formally appraised quality: no risk-of-bias assessment or GRADE rating is performed, so a labeled Level I source is not necessarily stronger than a well-conducted lower-level study. Apply clinical judgment and your organization's policies. Not a substitute for institutional protocols or provider orders. · EvidenceRN v0.5.2 · A project of AI + Nurse Academy
